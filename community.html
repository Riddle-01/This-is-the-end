Frequently asked questions

How do I join a club or team?

Browse the teams section, pick the one you like and hit “Join”. The organizer will receive your request.

Are athletes verified?

Profiles shown in this community demo are examples. PRIME will identify verified profiles when verification is enabled.

Can I create my own team?

Yes. Contact the PRIME team and tell us the sport, location and details of your group.

Is there a cost to join the community?

Community access is free. Individual teams may have their own fees, shown on each team card.
